Summary

Three things to fix first

Treatment pages are missing

Implants, Invisalign and emergency care share one general page, so none of them can rank on its own.

The business profile is incomplete

Opening hours, services and photos are missing, which weakens map results for nearby searches.

Mobile pages load slowly

Large images on the homepage delay the booking button on phones, where most local searches start.
